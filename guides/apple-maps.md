- The search field is in the sheet at the bottom ("Search Maps"); pulling the sheet up shows recents and saved places.
- A place's card shows its address, hours and a Directions button with the travel time.
- In directions, choose drive, walk, transit or cycle; each route shows its time, and Go starts navigation. From and To can be edited at the top.
- End Route stops navigation.
