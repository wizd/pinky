- For finding and reading things: bills, balances, services.
- Bottom tabs: 「首页」 Home, 「理财」 Wealth, 「消息」 Messages, 「我的」 Me (a video tab may sit between them).
- 「我的」 › 「账单」 Bills lists transactions by month. Its search and filter (spending, income, a month) narrow the list; a row opens the transaction's details: merchant, amount, time, status.
- Balance, 余额宝 and bank cards are under 「我的」. Amounts may be hidden behind an eye icon.
- The search box at the top of 「首页」 finds services and mini programs by name (utility bills, phone top-up, transport cards, government services).
- 「扫一扫」 Scan, 「付钱」 Pay and 「收钱」 Receive on 「首页」 deal in money: the Pay code pays whoever scans it. Paying and transfers ask for the payment password or biometrics.
- Launch often shows an ad or a pop-up with ✕ or 「跳过」 Skip.
