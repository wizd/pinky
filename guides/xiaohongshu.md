- Bottom tabs: 「首页」 Home, 「市集」 Shop (or 「购物」), ＋ Post, 「消息」 Messages, 「我」 Me.
- 「首页」 has 「关注」 Following, 「发现」 Explore and a local tab at the top; the magnifier at the top right searches.
- Search results have tabs such as 「全部」 All, 「用户」 Users and 「商品」 Goods, and sort/filter options.
- The feed is two columns of notes. A note opens with its pictures or video, title and text; scrolling down in it shows the date, tags and comments. Like, 收藏 Collect and comment are at the bottom.
- Pictures in a note swipe left and right; the dots show how many there are.
- 「我」 › 「收藏」 Collections holds collected notes; 「赞过」 Liked holds liked ones.
- ＋ posts publicly.
