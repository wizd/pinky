- "Search here" at the top finds places and addresses; choosing a result shows its card at the bottom with Directions, Start, Save and Share. Pulling the card up shows hours, reviews and photos.
- Directions: the travel modes (drive, transit, walk, ride-hailing, bike) are tabs at the top; routes list their times. "Your location" is the default start; tapping it changes it.
- Start begins navigation on the chosen route; X ends it.
- Bottom tabs: Explore, You (saved places, lists, timeline) and Contribute.
