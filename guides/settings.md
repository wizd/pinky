---
app: Settings
names: [Settings, 设置, 設定]
android: [com.android.settings]
ios: [com.apple.Preferences]
---
- The search at the top finds a setting by name faster than browsing (iPhone: pull the list down to reveal it).
- Android groups settings under Network & internet, Connected devices, Apps, Notifications, Battery, Display, Sound, Storage, Security & privacy, Location and System (languages, date & time, updates); About phone shows the model and Android version. Phone makers rename and regroup these.
- iPhone: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular, Notifications, Sounds & Haptics, Focus, Screen Time, General (About, Software Update, Date & Time, Language & Region, Storage), Display & Brightness, Battery, Privacy & Security, and each app's own settings under Apps at the bottom.
- Switches show on/off in their state; reading a setting needs no change.
- Some pages need the phone's passcode or the person's account to open or change.
