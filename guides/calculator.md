- Enter numbers by tapping the buttons; typing into the display does not work in most calculators.
- Buttons are labelled in words in the phone's language: plus/add, minus/subtract, multiply, divide, equals, point/decimal, clear (AC/C) and delete (⌫).
- The display at the top shows the expression and, after equals, the result. Google's Calculator previews the result below the expression as you type.
- Clear before a new calculation: the last result stays and the next operator continues from it.
- More functions (%, parentheses, square root, powers) are in landscape, behind an arrow or a calculator icon, depending on the calculator.
