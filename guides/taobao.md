- Bottom tabs: 「首页」 Home, 「消息」 Messages, 「购物车」 Cart, 「我的淘宝」 Me (a video tab may sit between them).
- The search box at the top of 「首页」 searches goods; the camera icon in it searches by picture.
- Results sort by 「综合」 Best match, 「销量」 Sales and 「价格」 Price (tap again to reverse); 「筛选」 Filter narrows by price range, shipping and more.
- A product page shows price, sales, shipping, reviews 「评价」 and the shop. 「加入购物车」 Add to cart and 「立即购买」 Buy now at the bottom both open a sheet to choose options (颜色分类, 尺码) and quantity first.
- 「购物车」 Cart: check items, then 「结算」 Checkout; the order page shows the address and total before 「提交订单」 Submit order and payment.
- Orders: 「我的淘宝」 › 「我的订单」, with tabs 待付款, 待发货, 待收货, 待评价. An order's 「查看物流」 shows delivery tracking.
- Full-screen promotions on opening close with ✕ or 「跳过」 Skip.
