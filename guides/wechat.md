- Bottom tabs: 「微信」 Chats, 「通讯录」 Contacts, 「发现」 Discover, 「我」 Me.
- The magnifier at the top of Chats searches contacts, groups, chat history, official accounts and mini programs. To open a chat, type the name and tap the result under 「联系人」 Contacts or 「群聊」 Group chats.
- In a chat the text field is at the bottom. When it shows 「按住 说话」 (Hold to talk) instead, the voice/keyboard button on its left switches back to text. 「发送」 Send appears after typing.
- ⊕ next to the field: photos, camera, location, files, transfers and red packets.
- Top-right ⋯ in a chat opens its info: 「查找聊天记录」 searches its history; in a group also members, name and notice.
- Long-pressing a message shows copy, forward, quote, delete and (for 2 minutes) recall.
- Moments: 「发现」 › 「朋友圈」. Mini programs: pull down on the Chats list, or search.
- Transfers, red packets and payments ask for the payment password or biometrics.
