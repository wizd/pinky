- 「首页」 Home: the city or location at the top left, a search bar, and a grid of entries: 「外卖」 Delivery, 「美食」 Restaurants, 「酒店民宿」 Hotels, 「电影演出」 Movies, 「打车」 Rides and more.
- 「外卖」 opens the delivery channel, with its own search for shops and dishes.
- A shop's page: categories on the left, dishes on the right. ＋ adds a dish; a dish with options opens 「选规格」 Choose options first. The bar at the bottom shows the cart and 「去结算」 Checkout.
- Checkout shows the delivery address (tap it to pick another saved one), delivery time, tableware, notes, discounts and the total. 「提交订单」 Submit order goes on to payment.
- Orders: 「我的」 Me tab, then 「订单」 / 「全部订单」 (some versions have an Orders tab at the bottom). An order shows its status and the rider's progress.
- Coupon and red-packet pop-ups are common on opening; ✕ closes them.
