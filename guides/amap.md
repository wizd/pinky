- The search bar at the top (「查找地点、公交、地铁」) finds places; a result opens its card with 「路线」 Route and 「导航」 Navigate.
- 「路线」 plans a route from 「我的位置」 My location by default: tabs 「驾车」 Drive, 「打车」 Ride-hailing, 「公交地铁」 Transit, 「骑行」 Cycle, 「步行」 Walk. Each route shows time, distance and, for transit, the lines and transfers.
- 「开始导航」 Start navigation begins turn-by-turn directions; 「退出」 Exit ends it.
- Home and work shortcuts (「家」, 「公司」) sit under the search bar when the person set them.
