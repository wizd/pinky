- Tabs for alarms, world clock, timer and stopwatch (iPhone: World Clock, Alarms, Stopwatch, Timers; Google's Clock: Alarm, Clock, Timer, Stopwatch, Bedtime).
- New alarm: ＋ on the alarm tab. Google's Clock shows a dial: the keyboard icon switches to typing the hours and minutes, with AM/PM in 12-hour time; OK saves. The iPhone shows wheels for hour and minute (and AM/PM); Save is at the top right.
- Each alarm has its own switch. Opening an alarm (iPhone: Edit or tap it; Google: the arrow on its row) shows repeat days, label, sound and snooze; on the iPhone, Delete Alarm is at the bottom of the edit screen.
- A timer takes hours, minutes and seconds, then Start.
- The time format follows the phone's 12/24-hour setting; check AM/PM before saving.
